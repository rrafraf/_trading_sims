What changed today

The slower-timeframe ladder is now in the experiment suite.

New 30m and 4h trend runs were added beside the existing 1h runs. Reference, Backtrader, VectorBT, PyBroker, and Muni all match on those new rows.

The report wording also now matches the stricter judge: a match means cash, position, equity, signals, and fill details agree.

Part Status Plain meaning

What is not proven yet

This still does not prove real broker behavior. Paper trading and live brokers have their own quirks.

We have not yet tested stop orders, limit orders, same-candle stop-vs-target ambiguity, queue position, latency, or market impact.
